Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

CoverCraft AI ("we", "our", "us") provides a free AI-powered cover letter generator, AI content detector, and text humanizer. This policy explains what data we collect and how it's used.

1. What we collect

  • Form inputs: job title, company name, your name, experience, and reasons for applying — submitted only when you use the generator.
  • Pasted text: any text you submit to the AI Detector or Humanizer tools.
  • Usage tracking cookie: a random identifier stored in a cookie on your device, used only to enforce daily free-usage limits. It contains no personal information.
  • Basic usage data: daily generation counts, stored locally in your browser (not on our servers) to enforce free-plan limits.

2. How your data is used

Form inputs and pasted text are sent to a third-party AI provider (Groq) solely to generate or analyze your text in real time. We do not store this content on our servers after the request completes, and we do not use it to train any AI model.

3. Daily usage tracking

We use a randomly generated identifier stored in a cookie, combined with your IP address, to track how many cover letters and humanizer words you've used today, and whether you've unlocked higher limits by watching ads. This data resets daily and is not linked to any personal profile.

3a. Advertising for unlocks

Watching ads to unlock higher daily limits is served through a third-party ad network. That network may set its own cookies or use its own tracking to serve and measure ads, governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to track your daily free-tier usage counts. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

5. Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

6. Third-party services

We use Groq (AI inference) to process generation and humanization requests, and Vercel (hosting) to serve this website. Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing data they process on our behalf.

7. Your rights

Since we do not maintain persistent accounts or stored profiles, there is no stored personal data to request deletion of beyond an email you've voluntarily submitted — contact us to have that removed.

8. Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on our homepage.
