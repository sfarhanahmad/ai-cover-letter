Frequently asked questions

Is this really free?

Yes — completely free. Up to 10 cover letters per day with no account, no credit card, and no hidden charges.

Will it pass ATS screening?

Yes. Letters are generated as clean plain text with no tables, graphics, or unusual formatting that ATS systems struggle to parse.

Do I need a different letter for each job?

Yes — and this tool makes that fast. Each letter is generated fresh with your specific company, job title, and reasons for applying.

Can I edit the letter after generating?

Copy it into Word, Google Docs, or any text editor to make changes. You can also click Regenerate to get a fresh version.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. Your inputs are sent directly to the AI for generation and discarded immediately. Nothing is stored on any server.
