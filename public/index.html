Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this really free?

Yes — completely free. Up to 5 cover letters per day with no account and no credit card. Watch a short ad to unlock 10/day.

Will it pass ATS screening?

Yes. Letters are generated as clean plain text — no tables, graphics, or unusual formatting that ATS systems struggle to parse.

Do I need a different letter for each job?

Yes — and this tool makes that fast. Each letter is generated fresh with your specific company, job title, and reasons for applying.

Can I edit the letter after generating?

Copy it into Word or Google Docs to edit. You can also click Regenerate for a completely fresh version.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. Your inputs go directly to the AI and are discarded immediately. Nothing is stored on any server.

What does the AI Humanizer do?

It rewrites AI-generated text to sound more natural — varying sentence length, removing AI buzzwords, and adding contractions and casual rhythm that reads as human-written.
